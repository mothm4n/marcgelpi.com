This guide is for leaders, OKR champions and people building internal support for change. It helps you explain why a bounded OKR pilot may be worth testing, prepare for objections and make a smaller ask. A full OKR introduction, company-wide rollout, compensation design and underlying strategy or ownership problems need separate work. For related experience, see [the Adevinta case on scaling OKR practice](/work/adevinta/) and [Marc's wider experience with organizational change](/about/).
