02

Practice before prescription

How I work

  1. Look at the system

    Start with intent, decisions, flow, feedback, and ownership. I use Grounded Theory techniques to code qualitative evidence from interviews, quantify recurring patterns, and move beyond a coach’s first impressions.

  2. Work laterally

    Connect leaders and teams across boundaries so shared problems can move without waiting for hierarchy. I also do the fieldwork: I am willing to get my hands dirty and work alongside people so they can see the practice in action. That closeness comes from having worked across development, product, and leadership roles, and from a generalist perspective that connects what specialists may see separately.

  3. Learn through change

    Make the smallest useful move, inspect the evidence together, and let the next decision earn its way.
